PLACEMENTS MODEL / 01

We sit between the household and the person.

The Chore Facilitator operates primarily as a placement agency.

Our role is to understand the household, define the role and help connect the client with a candidate who may be suited to that particular placement.

See the model
THE PLACEMENT MODEL

Three parts. One connection.

The process exists to connect a defined household requirement with a person who may be suited to the role, routine and working arrangement.

CLIENT

The household.

A family or household has a particular need, routine and set of practical requirements.

THE CHORE FACILITATOR

The facilitator.

We help clarify the role, understand the placement brief and consider suitable candidates against it.

CANDIDATE

The person.

An individual brings experience, availability, preferences and practical suitability for the role.

OUR ROLE

The middle matters.

01

Understand

Learn about the household, the type of placement required and the day-to-day environment.

02

Define

Clarify the duties, hours, language, transport, live-in or live-out preference and other practical needs.

03

Consider

Look at candidate experience, availability and suitability against the placement brief.

04

Connect

Bring both sides of the process together and move toward an agreed placement.

WHAT SHAPES THE PLACEMENT

The details define the fit.

Two households may request the same role and still need completely different people.

Duties, working hours, language requirements, driving, meal preparation, First Aid, travel, errands and household routine can all influence which candidate may be appropriate.
