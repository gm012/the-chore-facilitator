ABOUT THE CHORE FACILITATOR

Placement starts with understanding people.

01 / WHO WE ARE

The Chore Facilitator connects families and households with candidates for nanny, domestic, night nurse and housekeeper placements.

The focus is not simply on filling a role. It is on understanding the household, understanding the person and finding where the two may genuinely fit.

OUR POINT OF VIEW 02

A person is more than a profile.

A household is more than a job description.

The right placement considers both.

WHY WE EXIST

A more considered way to connect people.

Two families may be looking for the same type of role and still need completely different people.

Working hours, duties, language, transport, household routine, live-in or live-out arrangements and other practical requirements can all change what the right placement looks like.

Candidates bring their own experience, strengths, preferences and availability. The Chore Facilitator works in the space between those two sides.

INDIVIDUAL PEOPLE PLACEMENT

The role gives the process a starting point. The people and the environment determine the fit.

HOW WE THINK

The principles behind the process.

03 / APPROACH
01

Understand the household.

The placement begins with context: the family, the routine, the working environment and the support required.

02

Define the role properly.

Duties, hours, language, driving, meal preparation, travel and other practical requirements can all influence the placement.

03

See the person.

Experience matters, but suitability also depends on availability, preferences and how the candidate may fit the actual environment.

04

Bring the fit together.

The objective is not simply to make a placement. It is to bring the household requirement and the person together thoughtfully.
