ABOUT THE CHORE FACILITATOR

Good support starts with a conversation.

01 / WHO WE ARE

We help households looking for Nannies, Domestic support, Night Nurses and Housekeepers connect with people looking for that work.

We take time to understand what your home needs and what a candidate can offer. Then we help both sides explore whether working together makes sense.

OUR POINT OF VIEW 02

WHY WE EXIST

Getting to know you comes first.

Your family has its own routines, priorities and expectations. We want to understand them.

We ask about the work, hours, language and transport needs, as well as live-in or live-out preferences. This gives us a practical picture of the role.

We also listen to candidates: their experience, strengths, availability and the work they feel comfortable doing.
