Cookie Policy.

Digital tools should have a clear purpose too.

This policy explains how cookies and similar browser technologies may be used on The Chore Facilitator website.

COOKIE POLICY

01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store through a user's browser.

They can be used for purposes such as maintaining website functionality, remembering preferences, measuring usage or supporting third-party services.

Similar technologies may perform related functions without necessarily using a traditional browser cookie.

02

Current website use

The current website is intended to keep tracking and browser storage to the minimum required for the functionality actually being used.

Non-essential analytics, advertising or marketing technologies should not be introduced without first reviewing whether appropriate notice and user controls are required.

CURRENT BUILD

This website currently uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Cookie settings are available in the footer. If you use an online form, a temporary essential session cookie protects the submission. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts; this makes a connection to Google, but does not enable analytics or advertising on this website.

03

Essential technologies

Some browser storage or cookies may be necessary where they are required for security, user preferences, form handling, authentication or other essential website functionality.

Where a technology is genuinely necessary for a service requested by the user, it may not operate in the same way as optional analytics or advertising technologies.

04

Analytics

Analytics tools can be used to understand how visitors use a website, which pages are viewed and how the website performs.

If analytics services are added to The Chore Facilitator website, this policy should be updated to identify the relevant technology and explain how it operates.

Where consent is legally or operationally required before optional analytics are activated, those tools should remain inactive until the appropriate choice has been made.

05

Advertising and marketing cookies

Advertising or marketing cookies can be used to measure campaigns, build visitor profiles or personalise advertising.

The website should not describe such technologies as being used unless they have actually been implemented.

If advertising or marketing technology is introduced in future, the Cookie Policy and any required consent controls should be updated accordingly.

06

Third-party services

Some website functionality may rely on services provided by third parties.

Depending on how those services are configured, they may set cookies, process technical information or interact with browser storage.

Third-party integrations should be reviewed before launch so that this policy accurately reflects the technologies present on the live site.

07

Managing browser technologies

Most modern browsers provide controls that allow users to review, block or remove cookies and other stored website information.

Blocking technologies that are required for essential website functionality can sometimes prevent parts of a website from working as expected.

Where the website uses optional technologies that require an on-site preference or consent control, users should also be able to use that control to manage those choices.

08

Changes to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when the technologies or third-party services used by the website change.

The policy should be reviewed before launch and whenever analytics, advertising, embedded services or other tracking technologies are added or removed.
