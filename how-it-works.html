HOW IT WORKS 01 — 05

The right fit takes a process.

Placement doesn't begin with a list of available people.

It begins by understanding the role, the household behind it and the practical details that may determine whether a candidate is genuinely suited to the placement.

Follow the process ↓
THE PROCESS

From household requirement to working arrangement.

01 UNDERSTAND

Start with the household.

A role title alone does not tell us enough about the placement.

We begin by understanding the type of support required and how the role fits into the household's everyday routine.

ROLE

Nanny, Domestic, Night Nurse or Housekeeper.

ROUTINE

Hours, duties and the household's day-to-day.

ARRANGEMENT

Live-in, live-out and other practical requirements.

02 DEFINE

Get specific about the role.

Small details can completely change who may be suited to a role.

Language and literacy requirements, driving, school arrangements, meal preparation, First Aid, pets, errands, travel and working hours can all form part of the placement brief.

03 CONSIDER

Consider the person behind the profile.

Experience matters. Fit requires more than experience.

Candidate experience, availability, working preferences and practical suitability can then be considered against the placement brief.
