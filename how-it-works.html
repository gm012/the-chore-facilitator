Follow the process
THE PROCESS

A few clear steps, with a conversation along the way.

01 UNDERSTAND

Let’s start with your home.

What would make everyday life easier for your family?

Tell us which kind of help you need and a little about your daily routine.

ROLE

Nanny, Domestic, Night Nurse or Housekeeper.

ROUTINE

Hours, duties and the household's day-to-day.

ARRANGEMENT

Live-in, live-out and other practical requirements.

02 DEFINE

Work through the practical details.

Let’s make sure the expectations are clear.

We ask about hours, duties, languages, reading and writing, driving and school arrangements. Meals, pets, errands, travel and First Aid requirements can be discussed too.

03 CONSIDER

Consider candidates who may suit the role.

The work needs to make sense for the candidate too.

We look at experience, availability and working preferences alongside what your household needs. This does not guarantee a match.
